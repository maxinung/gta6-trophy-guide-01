Platinum Roadmap

Path to the Platinum Trophy: Vice Got a Price!

  1. Complete the five story chapters and all main missions
  2. Take every side mission and stranger strand as it appears
  3. Meditate at every spot, especially the Chapter 1 balcony spot with Lucia
  4. Finish all activities: Master each activity type, gold all shooting ranges, win all drone races
  5. Complete 100% Completion Checklist: bank vaults, gang operations, challenges, stunts, vehicle scans, animal studies
  6. Clear the entire map of Leonida
  7. Rob with your partner: 3 shops back-to-back, bank vaults, 4-star escapes
  8. Spend money: Dye packs, shop purchases, garage properties
  9. Register vehicles on Scooter Bros app
  10. Finish remaining challenges and collectibles for 100%
  11. Earn all other trophies to unlock Platinum "Vice Got a Price!"
