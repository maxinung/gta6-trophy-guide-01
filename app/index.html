Platinum Roadmap

The platinum trophy Vice Got a Price! unlocks after earning all 54 other trophies. The recommended order is:

  1. Complete Chapter 1 (Paradise Lost)
  2. Complete Chapter 2 (T**s Ass Drug Money)
  3. Complete Chapter 3 (Vice City, Baby!)
  4. Complete Chapter 4 (Top Of The World, Ma)
  5. Complete the final mission (You Gotta Know When to Get Off)
  6. Reach 100% Completion (A Long Road)
  7. Complete all activity types (Master of None)
  8. Earn gold medals in all Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist)
  9. Win drone races at all 14 locations (Top Fun)
  10. Complete 250 Stunts (Cunning Stunts)
  11. Complete 8 stranger mission strands (Strangers & Freaks)
  12. Complete 3 collectible card sets (Don't Touch My Deck)
  13. Earn $250,000 laundering marked bills (Dime on the Dollar)
  14. Spend $100,000 across all shops (Treat Yo Self)
  15. Purchase all 8 garage properties (Little Boxes)
  16. Register 10 vehicles to Scooter Bros app (On the Spot)
  17. Meditate at all 18 spots (Positive Mental Attitude) — do the Chapter 1 balcony session first!
  18. Find 100 vehicles (Whip It Good)
  19. Rob 5 bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal)
  20. Rob 3 shops back-to-back with partner (Retail Therapy)
  21. Complete 50 challenges (One Last Thing)
  22. Find all 52 areas (The Goodtime State)
  23. Make choices affecting Criminal Profile (Actions Speak Louder)
